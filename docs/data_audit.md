## Finding 1: `transfers_history.csv` is synthetic. **CONFIRMED**

- 14,990 rows, 4,334 unique names, built from only **78 first names × 58 surnames** (4,524 possible combinations; 96% of the grid is used). Real surnames are recombined at random (e.g. Diego Camavinga, Peter Camavinga, Quinn Camavinga, Tobias Camavinga).
- Most repeated name: "David Meyer" ×13. Of 3,793 names with >1 row, only **39** have a consistent implied birth year (year − age) across rows: rows are independent draws, not careers.
- Fees are hard-capped: max = €200m, with **382 rows at exactly the cap** and **1,003 transfers ≥ €100m** (the real market has had well under 100 such deals in history).
- Every year 2010–2026 has a €200m deal. Median fee by age is flat (€5.1m–€7.5m for ages 16–32); by position €5.3m–€6.6m. Real fees fall steeply after ~28 and vary strongly by position.
- Linkage to the 85 market-value players: 1 name(s), 3 rows (Eduardo Camavinga 2020 age 28 Napoli→West Ham €1.2m; Eduardo Camavinga 2023 age 25 Stade Rennais→Newcastle United €16.5m; Eduardo Camavinga 2026 age 25 Inter Miami→Galatasaray €2.5m). None match the real player's age or career.
- Structure is internally neat: 52 clubs, 10 leagues, 0 self-transfers, no missing cells. That neatness plus the above is typical of a generated dataset.
- Aggregate year trend (median fee €3.2m in 2010 → €9.4m in 2026) is smooth and could be a programmed inflation term; it is **not** evidence of real market conditions.

**Implication:** not usable at player level, and not trustworthy even for aggregate market context. Recommend dropping it (pending group confirmation of its provenance).

## Finding 2: market values are stylized (an age curve, not market data). **CONFIRMED**

- 85 players, 1,025 player-years, 2010–2026.
- Share of variance in log market value explained by **age dummies alone: R² = 0.33**; by **years-from-own-peak alone: R² = 0.86**; years-from-peak + player FE: R² = 0.95. A single template curve, shifted to each player's peak, reproduces almost all of the series.
- Median value by age: €10.4m at 18, €72.4m at 26, €14.2m at 30, €5.4m at 32. 79% of player-years at age ≥ 31 are under €10m, regardless of who the player is.
- Peaks: 86 rows flagged `is_peak_year` across 85 players; peak age median 24 (IQR 22–26).

## Finding 5: selection on the outcome. **CONFIRMED**

- Paid permanent fees: min €12m, 10th pct €45m, 87% ≥ €50m. Only 3 below €30m (Cristiano Ronaldo €15.0m, Aymeric Laporte €27.5m, Federico Chiesa €12.0m).
- The list is a curated 'record/notable transfers' list: the dependent variable is truncated from below, so OLS on log fee will understate every slope, including the commercial one.
- Several rows are themselves questionable and need checking against a public source before use: e.g. Désiré Doué PSG→Real Madrid €90m (2026-01-15) and Mathys Tel Bayern→Tottenham €50m (2025-01-05; publicly reported as a loan followed by a permanent deal in summer 2025).
