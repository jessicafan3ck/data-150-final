## 1. How club finances work

- The 52 clubs' combined revenue grew from €6.4bn in 2010 to €17.1bn in 2025 (+166%). Wages take a steady 54–56% of revenue.
- Net transfer spend is small next to revenue on aggregate (+3.0% of revenue per year on average), because one club's purchase is another's sale. In a typical year 47% of these clubs are net sellers.
- Across Europe's top divisions (UEFA), clubs sold €10.3bn of players in the summer 2026 window alone, against €28.6bn of operating revenue in 2024: roughly **€36 of player sales for every €100 of operating revenue**, and €6.8bn of that is expected to be profit. Player sales are a second income stream about a third the size of everything else a club earns.
- The supplied `transfers_history.csv` is not used for gross flows: it implies the median club sells about twice its annual revenue in players each year, which does not fit `club_financials.csv`.
